🛡️ Our Core Privacy Guarantee: ToolsBD is designed as an offline-first utility application. We do not collect, upload, store, or sell any of your personal files, photos, or documents to remote servers or third parties. All processing takes place strictly on your local device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolsBD ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user information and sensitive device data. By installing and using ToolsBD, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

To provide complete transparency, we emphasize that ToolsBD operates locally on your smartphone:

  • No Personal Identity Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user account registrations.
  • No Document or Image Uploads: Photos, images, Word documents (DOCX), and PDF files selected for compression, conversion, merging, splitting, watermarking, or editing remain 100% on your device. They are never transmitted over the internet to any external servers.
  • No Location Data: ToolsBD does not track, access, or store your GPS or network location.
  • No Financial or Payment Information: ToolsBD does not process financial transactions or collect credit card information.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

ToolsBD only requests device permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver its core offline utilities:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely when you use the built-in QR and Barcode scanner or choose to take a photo directly within the app. The camera feed is analyzed in real time on your phone; no video streams or camera recordings are saved or transmitted externally.
  • Storage and Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to allow you to browse and select photos, images, or documents stored on your device that you wish to compress, convert, or edit.
  • Save Files (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Direct Storage Access): Required to save your newly generated files (such as converted PDFs, compressed images, and generated QR codes) directly to your chosen device directory (e.g., Downloads/ToolsBD or Gallery).

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

ToolsBD prioritizes user privacy:

  • We do not integrate third-party advertising tracking SDKs that profile users.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third-party data brokers or marketing platforms.
  • Any local history (such as recently scanned QR codes or tool operation history) is saved exclusively in the app's private local database (Hive/SharedPreferences) on your own device and never synchronized with any cloud service.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because ToolsBD does not collect or store user data on remote cloud servers:

  • All generated files remain under your personal control in your phone's file storage. You can view, share, or delete them anytime using your native Android File Manager or Gallery app.
  • You can clear all app-internal history logs and settings at any time by clearing the app's cache/storage in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy

ToolsBD does not target or knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, ToolsBD complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

7. Security

We value your trust. By keeping all document and image processing strictly offline on your device, we eliminate the risks associated with cloud transmission, server breaches, and unauthorized interception.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to periodically review this page for any changes.
